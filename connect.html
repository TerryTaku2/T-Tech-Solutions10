What is T-Tech Connect

The Smarter Way to Find or List Property in Zimbabwe

Finding accommodation in Zimbabwe has always been a frustrating process — word of mouth, WhatsApp forwards, unsafe meetups, and no real verification of properties or landlords.

T-Tech Connect changes that. It is a dedicated digital marketplace where landlords list verified properties and tenants find accommodation safely, with photos, real pricing, and direct communication all in one platform.

Whether you are a university student looking for something close to campus, or a working professional looking for a furnished flat, T-Tech Connect makes the process simple, transparent, and trustworthy.

How It Works

Simple from Start to Finish

Getting started on T-Tech Connect takes minutes, whether you're a tenant or a landlord.

For Tenants
1
Create Your Account

Sign up in seconds with your email or Google account. Tell us whether you're a student or general tenant.

2
Search & Filter

Browse listings by city, price range, campus distance, room type, and furnishing. Find exactly what you need.

3
Message & Move In

Contact the landlord directly through our real-time chat. Agree terms and move in, no agents, no unnecessary fees.

For Landlords
1
Register as Landlord

Create your landlord account. It's free to register and takes less than two minutes.

2
List Your Property

Add photos, set your price, describe the property, and publish. Your listing goes live immediately.

3
Connect with Tenants

Receive messages from interested tenants, review their profiles, and choose who to rent to.
