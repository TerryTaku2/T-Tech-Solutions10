Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing Questions, Answered

Do I pay everything upfront?▾
No. I typically work with a 50% deposit to begin the project and the remaining 50% on delivery. For larger projects, I use milestone-based payment schedules so you pay as progress is made.
What currency do you accept?▾
I accept USD (cash or bank transfer) as well as ZIPIT and EcoCash. For international clients, I can accept payments via bank transfer. Payment method is agreed before the project starts.
Are there any hidden costs I should know about?▾
No. Your quote covers everything we agreed to build. The only additional costs that may arise are third-party services you opt into, like hosting platforms (Render, Heroku), domain names, or premium APIs. I always flag these upfront and you only pay for what you choose.
What happens if I need changes after delivery?▾
Minor revisions within the originally agreed scope are included. For significant new features or additions, I'll agree a small fee and timeline with you. All packages include a support period (1–6 months depending on tier) during which bug fixes and small tweaks are free.
Can I start small and expand later?▾
Absolutely, and I recommend it. Many clients start with a Starter package and upgrade as their business grows. I build everything with scalability in mind so expanding is straightforward and cost-effective.
How long does a typical project take?▾
A simple website takes 3–7 days. A full web application or ERP system typically takes 2–8 weeks depending on complexity. I always agree a clear timeline before starting and keep you updated throughout.
