Our Story

Finance Expertise Meets Modern Technology

T-Tech Solutions was founded on a straightforward belief: great technology should be accessible to every business, not just large corporations with big IT budgets.

We saw a gap in Zimbabwe's market. Businesses needed proper digital systems, ones built with an understanding of local realities, not just foreign templates applied without context. So we built a company that bridges that gap.

Today, we deliver custom web applications, financial systems, ERP platforms, AI tools, and our flagship property marketplace, T-Tech Connect. Every product we build is designed to solve a real problem for a real Zimbabwean business.
